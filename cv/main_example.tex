\vspace{6pt}
\small{Applied scientist with five years of experience developing, integrating, and field-testing autonomous maritime robotic systems (AUV/ASV) in applied R\&D at Fraunhofer IOSB. Combines a research background in physical oceanography (MPhil, University of Southampton; MSc Physics, University of Rostock) with hands-on engineering: ROS-based software, Python tooling, sensor integration (sonar, LiDAR, camera, navigation), and field testing across harbours, lakes, and rivers. Thrives on the full data pipeline -- acquiring data in the field, solving problems as they arise, and communicating results to stakeholders -- and is now broadening into GIS to expand into hydrographic survey work.}
